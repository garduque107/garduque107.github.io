Day 1 — Luke 2:19

January 29, 2026
“But Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart.” — Luke 2:19

This is the first principle of Selah Fragments: to keep, and to ponder.

Not everything needs to be explained immediately. Not everything needs a conclusion.

Some moments are meant to be held quietly, like fragments of light — stored with care, revisited with patience, and understood over time.

Today, I begin here: with remembrance, reflection, and a steady heart.
